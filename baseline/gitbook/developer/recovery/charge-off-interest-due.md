# Charge-Off Interest Due

Charge-off interest due moves any interest balance at the time of charge off to interest income charge off so accounting entries follow the defined accounting policy. Collections on charged-off interest balances post to the recoveries GL. Charge off applies to interest-bearing loans that use the progressive allocation strategy. From the time of charge off, no more disbursement is allowed. The charge-off transaction appears in the list of transactions.

OCA Repayment accounting mappings

| Portion | Maps to |
| --- | --- |

| Fees | Other receivables, recoveries, fund source /advance accounting, Income from Recovery payment |
| Penalty | Other receivables, recoveries, fund source /advance accounting, Income from Recovery payment |
| Unallocated Credit (UNC) | Other receivables, Other Credit liability, fund source /advance accounting, Overpayment Liability |

A loan has a principal of 1,000.00, an annual interest rate of 12%, and 3 monthly installments with 30-day months. The figures are illustrative. At charge-off, 30.00 of interest is due and unpaid. The system charges off this interest balance as part of the automated charge-off so the accounting entries match policy, and the charge-off transaction appears in the list of transactions. From the time of charge-off, no more disbursement is allowed on the loan.

This entry moves the due interest from the loan asset to the interest income charge-off account.

# Charge-Off Interest Due

Charge-off interest due applies to interest-bearing loans that use the progressive allocation strategy. When a loan is charged off, any interest due at that time is charged off to interest income charge off, and no further disbursement is allowed from the time of charge off. The charge-off transaction appears in the list of transactions.

An interest-bearing loan that uses the progressive allocation strategy is charged off while interest is still due. The figures are illustrative: the principal is 1,000.00, the annual interest rate is 12%, there are three monthly installments, and months have 30 days. After one month, 10.00 of interest is due. At charge off, that due interest is removed from the loan balance and posted to interest income charge off so the accounting entries follow the charge-off policy. From the charge-off date onward, no further disbursement is allowed on the loan. The charge-off transaction appears in the list of transactions. This entry shows that interest due at charge off is posted to interest income charge off rather than left in the loan balance.

| Account | GL type | Debit | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interest income charge off (404003) | Income | 10.00 |  |
| Loans Receivable (112601) | Asset |  | 10.00 |
| Totals |  | 10.00 | 10.00 |

After the loan is charged off, a payment of 5.00 is received on the charged-off interest balance. The collection is posted through the suspense/clearing account and then to the recoveries general ledger, keeping the recovery separate from ordinary interest income. Because the loan has been charged off, no new disbursement is permitted. This posting shows that recoveries on charged-off interest are routed to the recoveries GL.

| Account | GL type | Debit | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Suspense/Clearing account (145023) | Suspense/clearing account | 5.00 |  |
| Recoveries GL | Income |  | 5.00 |
| Totals |  | 5.00 | 5.00 |
